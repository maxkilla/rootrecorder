How it works

Four steps, tape to tree

Every step stays in your hands. Nothing reaches FamilySearch without your review.

STEP 01

Record

Sit down with a parent or grandparent and record a guided conversation, built around real life events — birth, marriage, migration, career.

STEP 02

Transcribe & extract

RootRecorder transcribes the recording and drafts candidate facts: names, relationships, dates, places, and events mentioned along the way.

STEP 03

Review & approve

You see every extracted fact before anything happens. Edit, correct, or discard — you decide what's accurate and what's ready to keep.

STEP 04

File to FamilySearch

Approved facts, relationships, and the recording itself are added to the person's profile in Family Tree as sourced, citable content.

FAQ

Common questions

Is RootRecorder affiliated with FamilySearch?

Not yet — we've applied to become a qualified FamilySearch Third-party Solution Provider, which allows approved apps to read and write Family Tree data. We'll update this page as that moves forward.

Do I need a FamilySearch account?

Yes. RootRecorder writes to your existing FamilySearch Family Tree — it doesn't replace it or require a separate tree.

What happens to the original recordings?

They're kept and attached to the relevant person's profile as a source citation and memory, so every fact they produced traces back to the conversation.

Who's building this?

RootRecorder is a project of CleverGhosts LLC, based in Lake County, California.

Want in on the first sessions?
